Bridge USDC

Circle CCTP v2 · burn on source, mint native USDC on destination.

How it works

1. Source wallet needs USDC and gas on that chain.
2. Burn creates a CCTP message.
3. Circle attests it (about 1–3 min on Sepolia / Base / Fuji).
4. Mint credits native USDC on the destination wallet.
